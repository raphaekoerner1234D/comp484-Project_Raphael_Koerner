Timeline of Persian Empire

The historical years of the Persian history

  • 550 BCE
    Rise of Cyrus the great
    Cyrus the Great defeats the Medes and establishes the Achaemenid Persian Empire
  • 546 BCE
    Conquest of Lydia
    Cyrus expands Persian control into Anatolia and defeats the Lydian kingdom
  • 539 BCE
    Conquest of Babylon
    Cyrus conquers Babylon, bringing Mesopotamia and the Fertile Crescent under Persian control.
  • 530 BCE
    Death of Cyrus the great
    Cyrus dies during a military campaign, and his son Cambyses II succeeds him
  • 525 BCE
    Conquest of Egypt
    Cambyses II leads the Persian conquest of Egypt
  • 522 BCE
    Darius I becomes king
    Darius I takes the throne and reorganizes and strengthens the Persian Empire
  • 515 BCE
    Persepolis
    Darius I develops Persepolis as an important royal center of the empire
  • 499 - 493 BCE
    Ionian Revolt
    Several Greek cities in Asia Minor rebel against Persian rule
  • 490 BCE
    Battle of Marathon
    Greek forces defeat a Persian army at Marathon during Darius I's campaign in Greece
  • 486 BCE
    Xerxes I becomes king
    Xerxes I succeeds Darius I and later continues Persian campaigns against Greece
  • 480 BCE
    Persian invasion of Greece
    Xerxes' forces fight at Thermopylae and later suffer a major naval defeat at Salamis
  • 479 BCE
    Battle of Plataea
    Persian forces are defeated by Greek forces, effectively ending the major Persian invasion of Greece
  • 334 BCE
    Alexander invades Persia
    Alexander the Great begins his invasion of the Achaemenid Persian Empire
  • 331 BCE
    Battle of Gaugamela
    Alexander defeats Darius III, severely weakening the Persian Empire
  • 330 BCE
    Fall of the Achaemenid Empire
    Darius III dies and the Achaemenid Empire falls to Alexander the Great. Persepolis is subsequently burned and looted

Source: Timeline of Persian Empire
